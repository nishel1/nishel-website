NISHEL community initiative

More connections. More possibilities.

We're building a support initiative for eligible, trusted lariwalas, rickshaw drivers and middle-class individuals who want to help new local ideas get online.

A small start can open another door.

Eligible participants may receive two free single-page websites before making a referral. After one successful referral, they may qualify for one additional free single-page website for a new startup.

What one page means
One single-page website for one specific business, service or type of work. This is website support—not cash, commission or a promise of income. Eligibility, approval, page scope, hosting, domain and ongoing maintenance terms must be confirmed with NISHEL before work begins. No automatic entitlement is implied.

Good to know

Questions, answered simply.

A clear understanding before we start helps everyone.

What does NISHEL provide?

We help create focused single-page websites for a specific business, service, creator or project, along with basic guidance to present the work clearly.

Is this a complete multi-page website?

The standard offer described here is one single-page website for one specific purpose. Additional pages or features need a separate discussion and agreement.

Are the community pages automatically free for everyone?

No. The community initiative is for eligible, trusted participants and is subject to review, scope and terms confirmed by NISHEL.

Are domain, hosting and future changes included?

These details depend on the specific arrangement and must be confirmed before work starts. Do not assume a custom domain, lifetime hosting or unlimited updates are included.

Can I share my page on WhatsApp and Instagram?

Yes. A published webpage link can be shared through messaging apps and social platforms.

Does this page offer unlimited AI generation?

No. This website is a lightweight static information and enquiry page. It does not promise unlimited AI generation or automated services that have not been built and connected.
