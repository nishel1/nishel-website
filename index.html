Community support

More connections. More possibilities.

NISHEL is developing a support initiative for eligible, trusted lariwalas, rickshaw drivers and middle-class individuals who want to help new local ideas get online.

A small start can open another door.

Eligible participants may receive two free single-page websites before making a referral. After one successful referral, they may qualify for one additional free single-page website for a new startup.

What “one page” means
One single-page website for one specific business, service or type of work. Eligibility, scope, hosting and any ongoing costs must be confirmed with NISHEL before work begins. This is a website-support initiative, not a cash commission.

How it works

Clear steps, human support.

01. Share your idea

02. Shape your page

03. Share it proudly

Once approved and published, use your page link across your channels.
